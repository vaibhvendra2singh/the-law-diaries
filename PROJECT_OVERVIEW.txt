1. PROJECT SUMMARY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Law Diaries is a high-legibility personal blog and digital legal journal 
designed for Samir Kapri (5th Semester Law Student at Bennett University) to publish 
weekly analytical essays, statutory commentary, and public policy research.

Tech Stack:
• Framework: Next.js 14 (App Router) with TypeScript
• Styling: Tailwind CSS (Monochrome Black & White Editorial Theme)
• Typography: Georgia / Source Serif 4 & Inter via next/font/google
• Database: Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL managed via Prisma ORM
• Authentication: NextAuth.js (Credentials Provider with bcrypt encryption)
• Markdown Engine: react-markdown & remark-gfm
• Email Notifications: Resend API
• Performance: Incremental Static Revalidation (revalidate = 5, sub-50ms loads)


2. FULL FEATURE LIST
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Authentication & Security
   • Single-Admin Access: Protected login for site owner (lawdiaries01@gmail.com).
   • Encrypted Password Storage: Passwords hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12).
   • Protected Routes: Admin pages (/admin, /admin/new, /admin/pages) automatically redirect unauthenticated users to /login.
   • Session Management: NextAuth.js JWT session handling with secure cookie storage.
   • Password Recovery: Automated password reset via /forgot-password & terminal CLI (npm run setup).

B. Content Editing & Management
   • Admin Dashboard (/admin): Stat cards (Total Posts, Published, Drafts), quick-action links, and Delete Post controls.
   • Split-Pane Markdown Editor (/admin/new, /admin/edit/[id]): Real-time side-by-side preview as you type.
   • Dynamic Page Manager (/admin/pages): Edit the title, subtitle, and biography text of About Us and Contact Us pages directly from the dashboard with 100% permanent file storage (content/pages.json).
   • Delete Article Control: Native confirmation dialog + Prisma cascading deletion (removes post + comments).
   • Slug & Metadata: Automatic URL slug generator, guest author name & affiliation fields, category tags selection, and excerpt fields.

C. Academic Legal Features (IndiaCorpLaw Standard)
   • 1-Click Bluebook Citation Box: Generates copyable academic citations ("Samir Kapri, 'Title', The Law Diaries (22 August 2026), available at...") with a 1-click "Copy Citation" button.
   • Guest Author Byline: Render bracketed author affiliation notes at the top of essays (e.g. "[Param Pandya is a Research Fellow at NUS Law]").
   • Recommended Reading Grid: Displays 2 related published essays at the bottom of post pages to keep readers engaged.
   • Legal & Editorial Disclaimer: Collapsible disclaimer box on essay pages & dedicated /terms page explaining personal capacity opinions, no attorney-client relationship, and third-party link disclaimers.
   • Academic Footnotes: Styled superscript numbers ([^1]) with horizontal divider line footnote bibliographies.

D. Public Blog Feed & Reader Experience
   • IndiaCorpLaw Feed Layout: Single-column vertical stream of articles with large serif titles, date lines, category tags, excerpts, and "Read more..." links.
   • Floating White Pill Header: Sticky floating navigation bar across top with logo glyph, uppercase links (HOME, ABOUT, CONTACT), and ADMIN LOGIN button.
   • Scroll Reading Progress Bar: Thin top bar tracking reading completion on essays.
   • Social Sharing & Copy Link: Clean text buttons (Share on X, LinkedIn, Copy Link with copied indicator).
   • Chronological Archive (/archive): Timeline catalog grouped by Year/Month with live search filter.

E. Reader Comments & Anti-Spam
   • Public Response System: Readers can leave comments under published articles.
   • Honeypot Anti-Spam: Hidden honeypot field catches automated spam bots silently.
   • Live Comment Updates: Submitted comments display immediately under articles.

F. Static Pages & Correspondence
   • About Page (/about): Author biography and academic background styled in serif typography.
   • Contact Page (/contact): Correspondence form delivering notifications live to lawdiaries01@gmail.com via Resend.
   • Terms & Conditions (/terms): Publication policies & legal disclaimer loaded from content/terms.md.

4. HOW TO PUBLISH & EDIT ARTICLES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to: http://localhost:3000/login (or your live Vercel URL).
2. Log in using your admin credentials (lawdiaries01@gmail.com / LawDiaries2026!).
3. Click "+ Create New Post".
4. Fill in:
   - Article Title: Headline of your essay.
   - Author Name: Samir Kapri (or Guest Author Name).
   - Author Bio: Optional affiliation note (e.g. "Research Fellow at NUS Law").
   - Excerpt: 1-2 sentence summary for homepage feed.
   - Tags: Comma-separated categories (e.g. "Constitutional Law, Policy").
   - Markdown Body: Write your article on the left; view live preview on the right.
5. Click "Publish Article" (or "Save Draft").

6. DEPLOYMENT TO VERCEL (FREE 24/7 HOSTING)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Push project to GitHub (`git push origin main`).
2. Sign in to Vercel.com with GitHub.
3. Import `the-law-diaries` repository.
4. Add environment variables from `.env` (`DATABASE_URL`, `DIRECT_URL`, `NEXTAUTH_SECRET`, `ADMIN_EMAIL`, `ADMIN_PASSWORD_HASH`, `RESEND_API_KEY`).
5. Click Deploy! Your live site URL will be generated in 60 seconds.
